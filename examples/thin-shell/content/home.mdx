This page is rendered by the Grove engine arriving as a **library mount** at
`/node_modules/@immediately-run/grove`, not by a fork of it. `src/App.tsx` is the whole
shell: it imports `GroveApp` and renders it.

The corpus exists so mode invariance is testable at all. `PLATFORM_LAYERING_SPEC §1.1` says
the same corpus renders identically under dispatch, fork and library composition — a claim
that needs a corpus in each mode to compare. This is the library one.

## What to look at

Nothing here is special to library composition, and that is the point: no frontmatter key,
no component and no link form is written differently because the engine arrived as a
dependency. Anything that IS different in only one mode is a contract bug.

See [the second page](/content/second.mdx) for the override in action.
